How the test works

  1. A white circle moves slowly around the screen. Keep your eyes on it the whole time.
  2. Sometimes a yellow square appears at the left, top or right edge of the screen.
  3. As soon as you see it, press the matching arrow button (◀ ▲ ▶) as fast as you can.
  4. Do not press before the square appears.
  5. The test has 3 short parts with a break in between (about 7 minutes in total).
Before you start: sit as you normally do, use the same device and hand as in your other sessions, and turn on Do Not Disturb so notifications don't interrupt you. You can stop at any time.
